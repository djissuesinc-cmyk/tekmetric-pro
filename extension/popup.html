Tekmetric Pro

These switches change how Tekmetric looks and types on this computer. They do not change Tekmetric's website for anyone else.

Sign-in

Your email and password stay in this browser. They are typed only into the Tekmetric sign-in page. They are not sent to Google.

Leave “Press Sign In” off if your shop uses a text code, or if Tekmetric asks you to prove you are not a robot. A wrong password is only tried once each visit, so it will not lock the account by retrying.

Writing help

The Google key is used only when you click Writing help on a Tekmetric page.

Leave a box blank to keep what is already saved. Get a key from Google AI Studio.
